LuaSec / OpenSSL payload bundled with REAL METAR
==================================================

These binaries let DCS World's Mission Editor make HTTPS requests (needed to
fetch real METAR data), something DCS doesn't support out of the box.

They are NOT built by the REAL METAR project. They are a pre-built LuaSec +
OpenSSL package compiled and maintained by the dcs-sms project:

    https://github.com/nielsvaes/dcs-sms
    (tools/me-mod/luasec/payload/)

Thank you to the dcs-sms author for compiling and sharing a working build.

Contents:

  lib/    -> installed to <Saved Games>\DCS\real-metar\lib\
             (ssl.lua, ssl/https.lua, cacert.pem, ssl.dll)
  bin/    -> installed to <DCS install>\bin\ and \bin-mt\
             (libcrypto-4-x64.dll, libssl-4-x64.dll, lua5.1.dll)

The bin/ files are only ever loaded by the Mission Editor's Lua environment,
never during actual multiplayer flight — but some servers' Integrity Check
could in theory flag extra files in the DCS install directory. See the main
README's "Requirements" section for details.
